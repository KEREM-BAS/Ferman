Annual Members’ Meeting 2026
Registration notice
The Annual Members’ Meeting of the Harbour Housing Cooperative takes place on Friday,
20 November 2026, at 18:30 in the community hall, 3 Quay Street.
Members who wish to attend are asked to register by 13 November 2026. Each member
may bring one guest. Please tell us in advance if you need step-free access or a sign
language interpreter.
The agenda covers the annual report, the accounts for 2025, the election of two board
members and the proposal to renovate the shared laundry.
This document contains scripts that check the registration details when it is opened in a
viewer that runs them.
