2.9Dark reading mode — inverted pagemacOS · Dark

Off by default; the page is paper-white in dark theme unless you turn this on. Text and backgrounds invert with hue kept, so highlights stay recognisable; photos and signatures are re-inverted to their real colours. Never applied when printing or saving.
