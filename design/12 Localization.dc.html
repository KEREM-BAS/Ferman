G34 · Numbers, dates, file sizes, paper sizes, units and percentages follow the region in Settings › Language and region, never the interface language. Digits come from the region: Saudi Arabia writes Arabic-Indic digits, the United Arab Emirates Latin ones. Numeric dates follow CLDR yMd (Germany writes 2.10.2026), times follow CLDR (Saudi Arabia ٢:٣٢ م, the United States keeps the narrow space before PM), and Türkiye puts the percent sign first (%100), which the zoom field and status bar now use. Boards that show another format are read as drawn for another region. Goldens fix the region per language: Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, and the United Kingdom for English.

12.1 · Main editor, Edit mode, same document state

12.1aEditor — TürkçemacOS · Light

Decimal comma (10,5 pt; 25,4 mm), “Sayfa 1 / 3”. “Organize” becomes “Sayfalar” so it can’t be confused with Düzenle (Edit).

12.1bEditor — DeutschWindows 11 · Light

The longest set of labels. The mode bar fits all eight modes; panel labels such as “Rechtschreibung prüfen” wrap rather than truncate; the status bar reads “Nicht gespeicherte Änderungen”.

12.1cEditor — العربية, full right-to-leftmacOS · Light

Window controls, tabs, sidebar and panel mirror; undo and redo arrows flip; Arabic-Indic digits in the chrome. The Turkish page is not mirrored and its marks (margin bar, frame label) stay on the page’s own start side.

12.1dEditor — 日本語macOS · Light

IBM Plex Sans JP with 20 px leading at 13 px; no synthesized bold or italic anywhere in the chrome. Japanese labels are shorter, so nothing truncates.
