03 · Edit mode — text

The core of Ferman: click into any paragraph and edit it like a document. Hover, caret, selection and reflow; the text-block panel; missing glyphs and font substitution; overflow; vertical Japanese. Continued in 03b · Edit mode — content.

3.1 · Edit text in place

3.1aEdit — Hover reveals an editable blockmacOS · Light
Click to edit · Madde 3

Only the block under the pointer shows its dashed frame, so the page stays readable; holding ⌥ reveals every block. The pointer becomes an I-beam over editable text and an arrow over images and fields. Keyboard: Tab walks blocks in reading order, each announced as “Text block, Madde 3, paragraph”.

3.1bEdit — Caret in a paragraph, typing reflows the blockmacOS · Light
Kiracı, aylık kira bedeli olan 19.500 TL’yi (on dokuz bin beş yüz TL) her ayın beşinci gününe kadar kiraya verenin aşağıdaki hesabına ödemekle yükümlüdür.

One click selected the block, a second placed the caret. The inserted words reflow both lines inside the frame with the original justification and font. The margin bar spans both changed lines. Screen readers: the block is a multi-line textbox; the caret position and edits are announced like in a word processor.

3.1cEdit — Selecting words inside a blockmacOS · Light
Kiracı, aylık kira bedeli olan 19.500 TL’yi her ayın beşinci gününe kadar kiraya verenin aşağıdaki hesabına ödemekle yükümlüdür.

Drag, double-click (word) or triple-click (sentence) inside an editing block. Selection is ink at 26%, never yellow. The floating bar acts on the selection only; the panel switches its fields to the selection’s values. Esc closes the bar, then leaves editing.

3.1dEdit — Main editormacOS · Dark

The page stays paper-white; editing marks keep their page-tuned colours. Chrome uses the soot neutrals and ink 500 as accent.

3.2 – 3.4 · Text-block panel, fonts and overflow

3.2Edit — Selected text block, all propertiesmacOS · Light

Text: family, size, colour (document colours first), bold, italic, underline, alignment. Spacing: character, line, before, after. Position and size in the region’s unit. Language per block drives spell-check and hyphenation. Every field is labelled; steppers take ↑ ↓ and ⇧↑ for ×10.

3.3aEdit — Missing glyphmacOS · Light

The block uses a subset font without ‘ğ’. The typed ‘ğ’ gets the amber wavy underline, the frame tab turns amber (label flips below, nothing above is free), the font field shows only the icon, the callout says it once. The thumbnail on page 1 carries the warning badge.

The frame keeps its size and the third line fades out under an amber dashed edge; “+1 line hidden” sits in the margin, never over the IBAN line. The panel offers three ways out. “Keep and warn” leaves a warning badge on the page and in Review edits (3.12) so it can’t be forgotten.

Ferman never fakes bold or italic. To emphasize, pick a font with a bold weight or use 傍点 (emphasis dots).

Columns run right to left; the caret is horizontal and moves down the column. “19” sits upright inside the column. Line breaks follow kinsoku rules so punctuation never starts a column. Bold and italic are disabled with a tooltip that says why.
