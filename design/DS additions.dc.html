FmWindow · document tabs and window chrome
Tab states: active (attached to the mode bar), idle, unsaved (dot replaces close), loading (spinner), locked, repaired; overflow “+3”. Windows: 46 px caption buttons, close turns #C42B1C on hover. Also new: FmSidebar, FmEditPanel, FmDocPanel, FmStart, DocKira and DocEkstre sample pages.
