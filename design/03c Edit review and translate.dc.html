This Agreement is made on 2 October 2026 between Northwind Analytics Ltd., 14 Harbour Street, Leeds, and Kaya Mimarlık A.Ş., Moda Cad. No: 18, Kadıköy, İstanbul (each a “Party”).

1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a joint bid for the restoration of the Haydarpaşa ferry terminal and will share confidential information for that purpose only.

2. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means any information disclosed by one Party to the other, in any form, that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

3. Obligations

The receiving Party shall keep Confidential Information secret, use it only for the Purpose and disclose it only to employees and advisers who need to know it.

Hartley’nin bu tespiti, Türk Borçlar Kanunu’nun 347. maddesinde düzenlenen bildirim süreleriyle birlikte değerlendirildiğinde, dönüşüm projelerinde kiracılara tanınan sürelerin yetersiz kaldığı görülmektedir.¹

Each block has its own language, detected on open and shown as margin tags when “Show block languages” is on (View menu). The English quote is spell-checked and hyphenated as English; the Turkish paragraph flags “kiracınnın” with Turkish rules. Screen readers switch voice per block (lang attribute written into the PDF tags).
