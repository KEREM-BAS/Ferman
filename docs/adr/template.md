## Context

What problem are we solving, and why now? What does the brief require (with section numbers)? What
does the design show (artboard ids)? Which facts constrain the choice: licences, versions, platforms,
performance budgets? Link the design gaps and questions that apply.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly. Use "we will" or the imperative. Give the exact names, versions and
rules someone needs to follow it. Say what is out of scope.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets easier, safer or faster.

### Negative

- What gets harder, what it costs, what risk we accept, and how we watch it.

### Follow-up

- Work this decision creates, with the milestone it belongs to.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative A.** What it is, and why we did not choose it.
- **Alternative B.** What it is, and why we did not choose it.
